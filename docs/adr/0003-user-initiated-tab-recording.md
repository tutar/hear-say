# Use user-initiated tab recording instead of platform media extraction

Hear & Say will capture sound from one user-selected browser tab through a generic, explicit Recording Session rather than parsing YouTube, Bilibili, or other platform media URLs, embedding their players, or offering video/audio downloads. Recording controls live in the Side Panel, only one source tab may be active, and completed or interrupted audio becomes a local Recording Draft before the learner edits and imports it; this preserves the existing three-surface extension design, avoids platform-specific extraction dependencies, and makes consent, recovery, storage, and transcription boundaries explicit.

The recorder will use optional permissions where possible, persist short chunks outside the Side Panel, and first validate a speech-oriented mono WAV path against real Chrome tabs and the configured FunASR service. Continued audible playback and valid sentence-level transcription gate product development. Long-duration stability, additional source compatibility, and forced-interruption recovery remain explicit Phase 2 validation work and gate experimental release rather than development.
